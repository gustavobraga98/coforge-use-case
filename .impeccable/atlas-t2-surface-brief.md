## Scope

T2 establishes the desktop-first foundation for the Atlas Travel demonstration. The surface is an Operate workspace for stakeholders viewing a fictional corporate trip; it must expose a conventional starting point and reserve space for the assistant without implementing the assistant workflow.

## Direction contract

THESIS: An orbital navigation chart makes the trip itself the workspace, refusing the generic card-dashboard arrangement.

OWN-WORLD: Midnight ocean surfaces, cyan route geometry, amber decision markers, restrained technical detailing, and quiet, high-contrast planning surfaces.

STORY: A stakeholder sees a real geography globe, understands that the route is the planning context, and can locate the visible Assistente IA control before any automation is enabled.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A minimal header spans the top; a large interactive Earth dominates the center; the São Paulo–New York trajectory provides the primary visual line; a compact planning rail or strip frames the work and a reserved right area anticipates chat.

FORM: Atlas orbital, third candidate in the grounded list, seed `7a75f06c`.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

## Constraints

The globe uses real local geography while route data remains fictional. Base React components accept structured props so future CopilotKit v2 GenUI renderers can reuse them. Keyboard operation, visible focus, responsive desktop/intermediate widths, and no external booking or mapping integration are required.
